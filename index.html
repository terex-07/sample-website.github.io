About Rahil

A journey from Vice Captain to Gold House Captain

Rahil Allaudin has proudly served the Gold House with dedication, discipline and responsibility. Beginning his leadership journey as the Vice Captain, he has now taken on the responsibility of serving as the Gold House Captain.

His journey reflects the values of teamwork, commitment, respect and perseverance. Through his involvement in school activities and house events, he strives to encourage students to participate, cooperate and give their best.

Being a House Captain is not simply a position. It is a responsibility to lead by example, represent the house with pride and help create an environment where everyone can contribute.
